Add `MaskedHeading` (`@zentauri-ui/zentauri-components/ui/masked-heading`): a semantic `h1`–`h6` whose words act as windows onto an image, a muted looping video, or a token-driven gradient mesh. Words rise out of their own line masks, or the media wipes or fades in, on mount or once the heading scrolls into view. Afterwards the media drifts slowly and slides under the letters with a mouse or pen. The entrance runs on one dependency-free animation loop that pauses off-screen. Reduced-motion preferences, forced colors and no-script environments all fall back to the final, readable heading.
